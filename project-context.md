## Implementation

- The static site is in `dist/`: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.mjs`, `carousel.mjs`, `projects.mjs`, `detail-content.mjs`, and `assets/`.
- `pdf-reader.mjs` renders responsive page images from `pdf-pages.mjs` in a single vertical scroll, with touch/wheel input, sticky controls, zoom/fit, reduced-motion-aware entry animations, and observer cleanup. `scripts/render-pdf-pages.py` regenerates the manifest and images from the public PDFs using Poppler, Pillow, and pypdf. The PDF test verifies source hashes, all 103 page entries, and both image variants.
- `carousel.mjs` holds wraparound, swipe, and mixed-width spacing rules so landscape and portrait cards remain separated as the carousel loops. `tests/carousel.test.mjs` covers the 15-card list, shape assignments, mixed-width spacing, and navigation.
- Ten media-only cards play a muted looping excerpt on hover or keyboard focus when reduced motion is not requested. The five projects without video previews use source images as still cards. Detail dialogs offer the full web video with controls where requested, plus source-grounded description and verified role; the tortoise project has a three-video selector, and the music video has separate completed-MV and behind-the-scenes choices. Five analytical/interview works use a full-width continuous document reader showing 103 pages across six public PDFs, with a small link to the original PDF. Jane and both Seoul Milk documents display only the original pages and reader controls; Teenoi/Lucky, Katsumidori, and MV Lam Pam retain their existing information below the pages. Seoul Milk opens a two-document choice popup before its reader.
- Project details use source-grounded Thai and English descriptions and bullet sections where they help the reader. The television seminar and Street Food keep their expanded descriptions without redundant detail headings, as explicitly requested. Piew Piew hides both the generic About and personal Role sections, placing its strategy in dedicated bullet sections below. The music video shows two Shotlist pages followed by three Breakdown pages, rendered from the Pages sources with no summary bullets. Each image keeps its full aspect ratio and opens at full size on click. Other sections use direct project prose without narrating what the slides say. Nested bullets group related facts where useful; verified individual contributions appear as bullets for other projects. On desktop the text column scrolls independently while the media stays visible on the left; narrow screens stack media and text in one scrollable dialog.
- The details were expanded from the supplied reports and presentation slides. Production work separates plans from finished media and reported shoot outcomes. The PR projects separate deck-reported measurements, proposed targets, campaign ideas, and any unavailable outcome data. Jane's detail view shows only her eight original report pages and family photographs, without an authored introduction.
- Teenoi/Lucky detail shows its 28-page public PDF rather than duplicate JPG slide controls. Katsumidori retains the slide-sourced persona/offer board below its nine-page public PDF. No measured campaign-outcome dashboard was present in that deck.
- Local preview: open `preview.command` or run `npm start` from this directory. Without `PORT`, the server listens on `127.0.0.1:4173`; with Railway's `PORT`, it binds `0.0.0.0` for deployment. The server serves PDFs as `application/pdf` and supports byte-range requests for PDF and video reading; `tests/server.test.mjs` checks those behaviors.
